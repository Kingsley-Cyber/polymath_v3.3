# Halveron Reliability Review

Voltage sag causes breaker trips on the eastern quay. Rejected manifests are consumed by the
Exception Desk. The Exception Desk produces the Correction Queue.

The Stowage Planner consumes Load Manifests. Each Load Manifest is derived from the Booking
Ledger. The Berth Fairness Charter defines berth fairness as the guarantee that no carrier
waits more than one tide cycle.

Norvale Group owns nearly everything on the south quay, according to a dockside joke. The
Stowage Planner depends on the Booking Ledger.
