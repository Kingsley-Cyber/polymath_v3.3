# Tidewatch Port Operations

Tidewatch Systems operates the berth scheduling stack at Port Halveron. The Berth Allocation
and Utilization Engine (BAUE) assigns arrival windows. The BAUE consumes the Tide Forecast Feed.
The BAUE produces the Daily Berth Ledger.

Dr. Nadia Osei leads the Quay Analytics Group. Dr. Osei owns the Berth Fairness Charter. The
Quay Analytics Group supports Dr. Nadia Osei with two reviewers.

The Cargo Manifest Reconciliation Service (CMRS) validates incoming manifests. The CMRS depends
on the Registry of Vessel Identities. Tidewatch Systems owns the CMRS.
